Rules

Goal

  • Clean the room by dragging laundry into the hamper and trash into the trash can.
  • Keep the cat from causing trouble while you sort everything.
  • If the cup falls and breaks, you lose.

Modes

  • Casual mode starts with a fixed mess. Sort all items to win.
  • Endless mode keeps spawning new items. Clean as many as you can before the mess meter fills.

Tools

  • Catnip sends the cat to a chosen reachable spot and distracts it briefly after it arrives.
  • The window distracts the cat at the window sill and takes priority over catnip.
  • Use these distractions to buy time when the cat is heading toward the cup or blocking cleanup.

Controls

  • Drag with the mouse to pan the camera and scroll to zoom.
  • Use W, A, S, and D to move the camera during gameplay.
  • Use the arrow keys to rotate and tilt the camera during gameplay.
  • Press C to start placing catnip.
  • Press Space to open the window.
  • Press P to pause or resume the game.
  • Press Esc to close this panel or quit to the main menu.
